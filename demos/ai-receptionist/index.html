No smoke and mirrors

What's real in this demo, and what's simulated

Working for real

  • Arabic & English understanding (Egyptian dialect)
  • Booking, rescheduling and cancelling logic
  • Free-slot search from opening hours and busy times
  • Double-booking and duplicate prevention
  • Answers only from the clinic's own information
  • Medical-advice guardrail and emergency handoff
  • Approval queue for every outgoing message

Simulated here

  • The calendar is an in-browser copy, not Google Calendar
  • Confirmations and reminders are never actually sent
  • "Busy" slots are sample appointments
  • Everything resets when you reload the page
  • AI phrasing is off; answers come straight from the clinic's info

Switched on per client

  • Google Calendar (service account) – adapter built and tested with mocks
  • Telegram bot – built and tested with mocks
  • WhatsApp Cloud API – adapter ready, needs the clinic's Meta account
  • Google Sheets CRM via n8n – workflows built and executed in sandbox
  • Claude / Gemini / OpenAI-compatible AI – optional, swappable
Under the hood

How it works

  1. Patient sends a messageWebsite chat, Telegram, or WhatsApp. Same assistant everywhere.
  2. Assistant understands & checksFinds the service, date and time, then asks the calendar what's actually free.
  3. Booking is createdEvent in Google Calendar, row in the CRM sheet, confirmation queued for approval.
  4. n8n does the back officeSends approved reminders, alerts staff about handoffs, emails a daily summary.
